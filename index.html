Questions

Before you book.

The things people usually ask on our first call. If yours isn't here, ask me directly.

Message me on WhatsApp
Can you redesign the site I already have?

Yes, and a lot of my work starts that way. I audit what's there, keep what's working, fix the structure and content, then rebuild the design. I set up redirects so you keep the Google rankings you've already earned.

Which platform should my site be on?

It depends on who will edit it, what it needs to sell and your budget. I build on Wix, Framer, WordPress and Squarespace, so I'll recommend the one that fits your team, not the one I prefer.

Can you connect my website to a CRM?

Yes. I set up GoHighLevel, ClickFunnels, Kajabi or Systeme.io so forms, bookings and purchases land in your CRM, get tagged and trigger the right follow-up automatically.

How long does a project take?

A focused landing page takes about a week. A full website usually takes three to five weeks. A CRM and automation setup usually takes one to three weeks, depending on how many workflows you need. You get a clear timeline before we start.

Will I own my website and accounts?

Yes. Your domain, website, CRM and every account are set up in your name. You get a walkthrough video at launch, so your team can make changes without calling me.

I'm in the US, UK or Australia. Will the time difference be a problem?

Not in practice. I'm on West Africa Time, which overlaps the UK workday and US East Coast mornings. Most of the work runs while you're busy, with a live call whenever you want one.

Questions

Before you book.

The things people usually ask on our first call. If yours isn't here, ask us directly.

Message us on WhatsApp
Do I have to hire all six teams?

No. Plenty of clients start with one service, like a new website or an email setup, and add more later. The advantage of one agency is that when you do add a channel, it already fits with everything else.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on what you need. After a free call we send a fixed quote with a clear scope, so there are no surprise invoices. Ongoing marketing runs on a monthly retainer you can pause or change.

How long does a website or app take?

A landing page takes about a week. A full website usually takes three to six weeks. A first version of an app usually takes eight to twelve weeks. You get a week-by-week timeline before we start.

What can you automate with AI?

Most repetitive work that follows the same steps: capturing and qualifying leads, replying to common questions, booking calls, sending follow-ups, updating your CRM and creating reports. We start with the tasks that cost your team the most hours each week.

Who will I be talking to?

A dedicated project lead who knows your business and joins every call. They coordinate the developers, designers and marketers behind the scenes, so you never have to chase five people.

Will I own my website, app and accounts?

Yes. Your domain, code, app store listings, email lists and social accounts are set up in your name. If we ever stop working together, everything stays with you.

How do you report results?

You get a short monthly report covering traffic, rankings, email revenue, social growth and app installs, with what we'll change next month. No jargon, no vanity metrics.
